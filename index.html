Welcome to Zubair's Homepage!

I am currently pursuing my Ph.D. in Computer Science at the University of Louisiana at Lafayette, USA, where I work as a Research Assistant at MedAI Lab under the supervision of Dr. Min Shi. Previously, I was a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Uttara University, Bangladesh, and a Remote Technical Writer specializing in Data Science and Computer Vision at Deepnote Inc., USA.

I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET), Bangladesh. My research focuses on Fair and Trustworthy AI Models, Causal Representation Learning, Bioinformatics, and Computer Vision, with an emphasis on robust and clinically meaningful machine learning for healthcare. I also enjoy writing about AI, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, and Data Science for a broader audience.

I am always interested in collaborating on challenging research problems in Medical AI, Computer Vision, Machine Learning, and related interdisciplinary areas.
